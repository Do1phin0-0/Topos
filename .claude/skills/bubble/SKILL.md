# Bubble

## Steps

1. Identify the scope — a single asset, a sector, or the broad market
   (default to broad market if unspecified).
2. Gather bubble-indicator evidence: valuation vs. historical norms,
   leverage/margin debt levels, speculative volume (options activity,
   meme/retail flow), breadth of participation, and narrative extremity
   ("this time it's different" rhetoric).
3. Weigh the evidence for and against a bubble framing — don't confirm
   the premise by default just because the command was invoked.

## Output

- Indicators checked and what each shows
- A calibrated conclusion (e.g. "elevated valuation but not extreme
  leverage" rather than a binary yes/no)
- Disclaimer: this is a framework-based read, not a market-timing call.
