# News

## Steps

1. Search recent (last 12-24h) headlines across major financial outlets
   (Reuters, Bloomberg, WSJ, CNBC, Financial Times), plus sector-specific
   trade press if the user named a sector or asset.
2. Filter to stories with genuine market impact — moved prices, changed
   guidance, regulatory action, macro data — over generic chatter.
3. Rank by magnitude of impact and breadth of relevance.
4. Present as a numbered list of 5-7 items: headline, one-line "why it
   matters," and source + timestamp.

## Notes

- This is a news summary, not investment guidance.
- Flag if a story is developing/unconfirmed rather than settled fact.
