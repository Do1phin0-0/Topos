# Risk

## Steps

1. Research regulatory exposure: pending litigation, antitrust action,
   sector-specific regulation.
2. Research financial risk: debt load, liquidity, refinancing walls,
   margin trends (or, for crypto, treasury/runway and protocol solvency).
3. Research competitive risk: market share trends, new entrants,
   disruption threats.
4. Research macro sensitivity: rate sensitivity, FX exposure,
   cyclicality.

## Output

- Categorized risk list (Regulatory / Financial / Competitive / Macro),
  each rated qualitatively (Low/Medium/High) with the specific evidence
  behind the rating.
- Closing note: this is a risk inventory, not a verdict on whether to
  hold or avoid the position.
