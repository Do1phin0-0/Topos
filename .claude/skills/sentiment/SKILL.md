# Sentiment

## Steps

1. Search recent news tone, analyst commentary, and social/forum
   chatter (where accessible) for the asset.
2. Characterize sentiment (bullish / bearish / mixed) and, importantly,
   whether it's shifting — improving or deteriorating — and why.
3. Distinguish retail sentiment from institutional/analyst sentiment
   when they diverge; that gap is often the most useful signal.

## Output

- Overall sentiment read
- Supporting evidence
- Notable divergences (retail vs. institutional, or sentiment vs. price
  action)
- One-line disclaimer: sentiment is a data point, not a signal to act on
  alone.
